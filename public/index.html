Rules and policies

Gameplay rules
  1. Card values: 2 to 10 count as their number, J is 11, Q is 12 and K is 13. Suits do not matter.
  2. An Ace counts as 1 or 14. When you are dealt an Ace, the game picks the value that gives you the wider gap and shows it under the card: with a 2 to 7 the Ace counts as 14, with an 8 to K it counts as 1. Two Aces count as 1 and 14.
  3. If the next card is an Ace, it matches your Ace if you hold one. Otherwise it is outside your two cards.
  4. You get 3 tries a day. Each try deals you two cards from a full, freshly shuffled deck of 52. Your two cards always have at least one rank between them.
  5. Before you choose, the game lists exactly which next cards score +100, which score the +200 double and which cost 100.
  6. Choose "Go for it!" or "Skip". Either choice uses the try, and the next card is shown both ways.
  7. You have 10 seconds to choose. If you do nothing, the game goes for it for you.
  8. Your account can be used on one device only. Signing in on a new device needs a code sent to your email. There is no sign-out: if you sign in on another device, the earlier device is signed out and play is paused for 1 hour, even if you have simply changed phones.
  9. If you go for it: a card strictly between your two cards scores +100 points. A card of the same rank as either of your cards is a double: it scores +200 points and the try is given back, so you play again. Any other card costs 100 points, but your score never goes below zero.
  10. If you skip, your points do not change and the try is used, whatever the next card is.
  11. Every point players score comes out of the banker's ang pow. When it is empty the banker has fallen and the game closes until tomorrow. A hand you have not yet decided on is cancelled and its try is given back.
  12. Gold dots are today's tries and expire at the end of the day. Every invited friend gives you one extra try. You can use at most 5 extra tries a day (the green dots); the rest carry forward. Extra tries are used first, then today's 3 tries.
  13. Trial run, 16 January to 5 February 2027: your score resets to zero every day, with a daily leaderboard.
  14. Final showdown, 6 to 20 February 2027: every point adds to your total, and the showdown leaderboard ranks players by that total.
  15. Points are only for the leaderboard. They cannot be bought or cashed out.
